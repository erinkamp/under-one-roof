My Home Files

Your home, a little more organized. Save links to files you already keep in your own cloud storage.

Privacy in this prototype: Saved links and notes stay in this browser on this device only. They are not synced, backed up, encrypted as a secure vault, or visible to the app creator. Don't save passwords, Social Security numbers, account numbers, or sensitive document contents here. Keep the original documents in your trusted storage service and check sharing permissions before saving links.

Quick tip

In Google Drive, iCloud Drive, or OneDrive, find the file's sharing or copy-link option. Keep it private or restricted to your own account.
