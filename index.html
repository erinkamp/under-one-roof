Upload home documents directly from your device, or keep using links to files stored elsewhere.

Privacy: Uploaded documents are stored in private storage and tied to your signed-in account and selected property. Keep your original files as backups. Do not upload passwords, Social Security numbers, banking information or other highly sensitive records.

A little local inspiration, whether you've lived here forever or just got the keys. Explore neighborhoods, parks, shops and community resources across Central Iowa.

The neighborhood notebook · Late fall

A few good reasons to get out of the house

Not everything worth exploring requires a ticket, a reservation, or an elaborate plan.

Take the scenic route

Bundle up for a walk on Clive's Greenbelt Trail. Even a short stretch counts, and it's a good excuse to notice how the neighborhood changes with the seasons.

Make an afternoon of it

Pick a couple of independent storefronts in Valley Junction or the East Village and wander without an agenda. You might find a new favorite.

Look a little closer

Walk a few blocks in Sherman Hill or Beaverdale and notice the architecture. Central Iowa has plenty of interesting homes beyond the usual new-build tour.

These are evergreen outing ideas, not a calendar of confirmed events. Check individual destinations for hours and accessibility.
