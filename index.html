My Home Files

Your home, a little more organized. Save links to files you already keep in your own cloud storage.

Privacy in this prototype: When signed in, saved links and notes sync to your private homeowner account. Signed-out test records remain on this device only. This is not a secure document vault or a guaranteed backup. Don't save passwords, Social Security numbers, account numbers, or sensitive document contents here. Keep the original documents in your trusted storage service and check sharing permissions before saving links.

Prototype privacy: These records stay only in this browser on this device, without account sync or secure vault encryption. Avoid entering serial numbers or sensitive receipt details for now. Use private cloud links and keep originals in your trusted storage.
