Privacy & account help

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Under One Roof is a free homeowner organizer independently operated by Erin Kamp. It is a homeowner organization tool, not a real estate brokerage, and does not provide real estate brokerage services. This page explains how the app handles information. The service is evolving and uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.

What you choose to save

If you create an account, the app stores your email for sign-in, property nicknames and addresses, home history, appliance information, home contacts, reminders, document links, and documents you upload. This information is used to provide the organizer and synchronize it across devices. Some signed-out sample records may be stored locally in your browser.

Where information is stored

Account information and homeowner records use Supabase. Uploaded files are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket with account- and property-based access rules. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may process technical usage information as part of providing the service. Do not upload passwords, Social Security numbers, banking information, or other highly sensitive records.

Who can see it

Your signed-in records are intended to be accessible only to your account, subject to the app's access controls. You can choose to export selected home history to share with another person; exports are files you control. Anyone with access to your signed-in device or an exported file may be able to see its contents.

Keeping your records

Keep original copies of important documents. The service has no promised uptime or recovery time, and its free hosting tier does not provide automatic database backups. You can export home-history and appliance records from the app; these exports do not include the bytes of uploaded documents. Download important uploads individually.

Account deletion or privacy questions

To request deletion of your account and its associated records and uploaded files, email the app operator at erinkamprealtor@gmail.com (current account support address). Include the email address used for the account, but do not send your password. Deletion requests currently require manual verification and processing; deleting an account from Supabase also requires its owned storage objects to be removed first. We will confirm when the request has been completed. Do not assume that signing out deletes your information.

Service limitations

Features and data handling may change as the app is improved. No advertising tracker or third-party analytics is intentionally configured in the current app code. External resources linked from the app have their own privacy practices.

For urgent questions about the safety or availability of your saved records, stop uploading new information and keep copies of your originals.
